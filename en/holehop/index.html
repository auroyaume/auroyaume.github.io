Hole Hop 3D Mini Golf

A 3D mini golf game you play with your finger: aim, shoot, and watch the ball bounce, slide, climb or drop. Short rounds, courses that get trickier and trickier.

Free · Android · English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, Italian

Screenshots

A look at the courses

How to play

Easy to pick up

Aim

Slide your finger to pick a direction. The right angle often matters more than power.

Shoot

Judge your shot, then watch the ball bounce, slide across the ice or fly off a jump.

Find the secrets

Some walls hide passages. Find them to earn purple stars.

The worlds

Every world has its own ideas

Its own look, its own traps and its own mechanics. New worlds unlock as you earn stars.

And a hidden world, for those who look closely.
