Delete your Hole Hop data

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Hole Hop is a mobile minigolf game published by Au Royaume (Google Play developer “Au Royaume”). The game has no account: here is how to delete the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to auroyaume.contact@gmail.com with the subject “Hole Hop – data deletion”.
  2. If you sent a bug report, give its date, the game version and the beginning of your text so we can find it.
  3. We reply and delete what we hold within 30 days.

What you can delete yourself, right away

  • Progress, coins, items and settings: stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them for good.
  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): turn them off in the game, Settings → Usage statistics. They are linked to an installation ID, not to your name; uninstalling the game resets this ID.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted on request: your bug reports (stored by Au Royaume in Airtable).
  • Deleted automatically: usage statistics, at the end of Google Analytics' retention period (14 months at most).
  • Kept: purchase records, kept by Google Play for legal and accounting reasons (Au Royaume only receives the confirmation of a purchase). Ad data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Full details in the privacy policy.
